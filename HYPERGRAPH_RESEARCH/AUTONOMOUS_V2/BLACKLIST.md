# Blacklist

The following mechanisms are closed for this research loop:

- PCHR (Pairwise-Complementary Hypergraph Residual).
- LCHR (Link-Conditioned Hyperedge Routing).
- Random Top-K hyperedge sampling.
- Candidate-conditioned hyperedge routing or simple candidate attention.
- Any method that deletes or sharply limits the set of hyperedges used for propagation.

Do not revive these under a new name. The working principle for this loop is to preserve the full global hypergraph and test calibrated messages or support objectives.

The bounded V2 screen also rejected SSHC cohesion calibration, RAHC overlap calibration, and HSA leaf-support alignment under their registered controls and effect-size rules. Do not continue this loop with a fourth mechanism or reinterpret a shuffled-control result as a candidate win.
