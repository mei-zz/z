# Idea ledger

All 14 V12 ideas have completed falsification and are marked rejected. No candidate survived formal promotion against the strongest fixed QTHS25 baseline; initial SH75-only F1/D1 signals were provisional and failed matched QTHS25 confirmation, so Phase 2 did not start. Six broad mechanism families were attempted (training dynamics/positive-side learning, pair/decoder, context, graph–hypergraph representation, ranking objective, and consistency regularization). Phase 1 used 39 of the 40 Stage-1-equivalent training-run budget; one equivalent remains unused because it cannot fund a new complete candidate/matched-control pair. Persistent teacher-hardness was deferred because archived teacher artifacts contain frozen final scores rather than multiple checkpoint score trajectories. Blacklisted CODNS/CPTS mechanisms were not renamed. There are no active candidates.

Scoring is 1–5, higher is better except engineering cost (5 is cheaper). Scores order the screen, not claims of novelty or results.
