The R-HSPE collision criterion requires the joint construction: (1) a pairwise candidate (u,v); (2) tokens enumerating incident hyperedge pairs through shared support w, with real identity multiplicity; (3) training-only ECDF cardinality descriptors; (4) permutation-invariant learned token encoding and mean/max pooling plus explicit counts; (5) decoder-level residual. Sharing one ingredient is conceptual overlap, not an exact implementation collision.

## Evidence notes and closest overlap

OFSH's inspected method and ablation passages use order-conditioned neighborhood aggregation and max-min extraction in its encoder. These support overlap in cardinality awareness and pooling, while the described representation unit differs from R-HSPE's candidate-specific incident-hyperedge-pair multiset. NSLR-HMANN official attention.py computes hyperedge embeddings with H.T @ X followed by learned attention; train.py reconstructs node relations rather than constructing the frozen R-HSPE token descriptors. NCN/NCNC already establishes learned candidate common-neighbor pooling; candidate-specific context encoding by itself is not a defensible first-use claim.

CCLPH's primary method Eq9 explicitly scores a two-node candidate using incident hyperedge intersections and their sizes s1,s2, with an analytic size-product denominator within communities. This is meaningful prior overlap in candidate-specific hyperedge-pair/cardinality context. It prevents a broad claim that R-HSPE introduced that context category. The inspected CCLPH construction does not contain the frozen training-only ECDF size-pair descriptors or learned permutation-invariant residual set encoder.

HCM uses two-edge size and intersection statistics in a generative model. HNHN and HGNN already normalize by hyperedge cardinality. HP2PH also incorporates non-uniform hyperedge size information. None of those ingredients should be described as first-use innovations.

HMRLH is the 2023 Electronics method, not the similarly named 2024 LEHMN manuscript or the KDD2025 Hypergraph Motif Representation Learning paper; all are distinguished here to avoid acronym/title conflation. HyperJoin's arXiv HTML has an embedded PVLDB2024 template reference inconsistent with its 2026 arXiv identity; this audit uses only arXiv2601.01015 as the bibliographic identity and does not adopt the embedded DOI as verified publication metadata.

Search absence does not prove absence of publications. Subscription abstracts and inaccessible full-text sections have UNKNOWN/NR fields; the overall status means no exact collision was identified in the accessible evidence, not that all potential collisions have been conclusively excluded.

## Claim boundary

Candidate formulation: R-HSPE constructs an incident-hyperedge-pair multiset for each pairwise candidate and learns a permutation-invariant decoder residual using training-only rank-normalized cardinality descriptors and explicit counts.

This describes the frozen method and the narrow combination assessed here. Do not claim first use of hyperedge size, candidate hyperedge-pair context generally, order-aware prediction, or rank normalization. Do not attribute observed gain causally to cardinality. The V17.1 mechanism remains CONTEXT_DRIVEN / SIZE_CAUSAL_CLAIM_NOT_SUPPORTED. Training-only ECDF controls descriptor scale by construction; reduced cross-graph sensitivity is a hypothesis, not an isolated experimental result. Test support confirms prediction generalization under this frozen protocol, not novelty priority.
