# HSPE definition

For a candidate pair (u,v), retain the multiset of hyperedge-size pairs generated by the exact V16 C1 procedure over shared supports. Each hyperedge pair is represented by the frozen symmetric size-only token already used by V16. The V16 size-only token encoder, pooling, explicit count inputs, residual head, and zero initialization are unchanged.

If an incident hyperedge has cardinality n, define s=log(1+n). For the two hyperedges, use [s1+s2, |s1-s2|, s1*s2]. V16 six-wide token slots for overlap descriptors are set to zero. No overlap descriptor is read by HSPE-REAL.

The candidate representation is the concatenation of the mean and elementwise maximum of ReLU(Linear(token)), followed by log(1+token_count) and log(1+shared_support_count). A zero-initialized linear residual is added to the frozen Raw-HG DCDLP baseline score. The V16 C1 implementation adds 75 parameters, subject to the V17 limit of at most 1% of baseline parameters.

The V17 claim under test is candidate-specific and set-valued: whether explicitly encoding the size-pair multiset improves over the baseline, a scalar histogram summary, a size-multiset shuffle, and a parameter-matched constant-token encoder across Cora and PubMed seeds 0/1/2.
